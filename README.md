## FAQ

**1) What’s the real difference between a VPN and a proxy?**  
A VPN routes *all* traffic through an encrypted tunnel. A proxy usually routes *one app* (or one device setting) and typically doesn’t encrypt.

**2) Does a proxy hide me like a VPN?**  
Not in the same way. Without AES-256 encryption and a Kill Switch, a proxy can leak traffic and metadata more easily.

**3) Why does a Kill Switch matter?**  
If the connection drops, a Kill Switch blocks traffic so your real IP doesn’t spill during reconnects.

**4) Will a VPN slow me down?**  
There’s overhead, but good providers keep **high speed** with **stable connection**—especially when you pick a close server from a **Global Servers** list.
